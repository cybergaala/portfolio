From August 1 to 6 we went to Massachusetts to visit my dad and meet my two half-brothers.

It was a trip full of first times. Our daughter's first trip, at one year and five months old. My partner's first time in the United States. And the first time we were all together: my brothers live in Canada and drove seven hours to spend two days with us and our dad.

Every photo was taken with the [Fujifilm X-M5](/blog/fujifilm-x-m5).
