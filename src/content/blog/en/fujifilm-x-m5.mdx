## Why a camera

I'm not a photographer. Until now, every photo I took was with my phone. But my daughter is growing up fast, and I wanted something special to keep those moments, photos I can look at in twenty years and still love.

I also wanted a camera for traveling abroad. Phone photos are fine, but I wanted something that made me stop and actually look at the place I'm visiting.

## Unboxing

The camera arrived in April 2025. My cat checked the box before I could open it.

![The Fujifilm box on the bed, with my cat checking it out](../../../assets/blog/fujifilm-xm5/01.webp)

I chose the silver one because it looks like an old film camera. Honestly, that was a big part of the decision.

![The silver X-M5](../../../assets/blog/fujifilm-xm5/02.webp)

## Learning as I go

It has a lot of dials and buttons, and I still don't know what most of them do. My favorite is the one on the left: it changes the colors of the photo to look like old film. I just turn it until I like what I see.

![Top of the camera with its dials](../../../assets/blog/fujifilm-xm5/03.webp)

The other dial has an Auto mode, and to be honest, that's where it stays most of the time.

The screen flips out, which helps a lot when I'm on the floor taking photos of my daughter.

![Back of the camera with the flip screen](../../../assets/blog/fujifilm-xm5/04.webp)

## The best part

It's small and light, so I actually take it with me. It lives on my desk, ready to go.

![The X-M5 on my desk](../../../assets/blog/fujifilm-xm5/05.webp)

And this is why I bought it.

![My daughter smiling, on the camera screen](../../../assets/blog/fujifilm-xm5/06.webp)
